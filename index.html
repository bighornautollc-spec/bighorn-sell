Questions

Frequently asked questions

How fast is "same day"?

Submit your vehicle during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9–5) and you'll usually have our offer within a few hours — same business day. Requests received after hours are answered first thing the next business morning.

Who determines the offer?

Every vehicle is appraised personally by our buying team using current market data and actual Montana sale prices — no automated estimates. If you have a number in mind, include it in the form and we'll address it directly.

Is the offer final?

Your offer is based on the details and photos you submit, and it's confirmed with a quick in-person inspection at the lot. If the vehicle matches what you sent, that's the number you're paid. If the inspection turns up something the photos didn't show, we'll walk you through any adjustment before anything is signed.

Why do you ask for photos?

Five quick photos — the exterior corners, the interior, the odometer, and any damage — help us price your vehicle accurately up front, so the in-person inspection is quick and your offer holds.

Do I have to buy a car from you?

No. We buy vehicles outright. If you'd rather trade toward something on our lot, that works too — same offer either way.

What do I bring to the lot?

The title (or your loan payoff information), a photo ID, and all the keys. If a lender holds the title, bring your account details and we'll handle the payoff.

My car doesn't run — will you still buy it?

In most cases, yes. Submit the details through the form and note that it doesn't run — we'll respond with what we can offer.
